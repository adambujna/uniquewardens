<h1 align="center">Unique Wardens Mod</h1>

![Minecraft](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft-26.3-3DCBE8)
![Loaders](https://img.shields.io/badge/Loaders-Fabric%20%7C%20NeoForge-6b6b35)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC0--1.0-lightgrey)

The purpose of this mod is to make the Warden feel more unique and make fighting it more rewarding.
Each Ancient city can only have one Warden.
This means that multiple Wardens will not spawn in the same Ancient city simultaneously
and if the Warden of an Ancient city gets killed, the Ancient city dies and goes quiet forever.
This is unless you respawn its Warden back with a ritual.
This functionality was added so that you may fight the Warden as many times as you want
without searching for a new Ancient city each time.

## Features

- **One Warden per Ancient city** – shriekers in an Ancient city won't summon a new Warden while one is already alive there.
- **Better loot** – the Warden now drops much better loot including Enchanted golden apples and Bottles o' Enchanting.
- **Permanent slaying** – defeat the Warden and that Ancient city is cleared. Its shriekers won't shriek anymore.
- **A ritual to undo it** – to fight the Warden again without searching for a new Ancient city you can summon it back with a ritual.
- **Consequences either way** – kill the ritual's Warden and the city is cleared again. Let it wander off instead, and the city wakes back up.

## How to re-summon the Warden

> *Note: Only works inside an Ancient city whose warden has already been killed. If the Warden is still alive,
> or hasn't spawned yet, you have to deal with it normally first.*

If you wish to fight the Warden of an Ancient city again after defeating it once,
you have to spawn and reactivate the city using a simple altar similar to the Wither or the Ender dragon.
If the warden spawns using this ritual, the city it is spawned in is brought back to life which means that Shriekers
shriek again, and if the Warden buries itself, it will remain active and can be normally summoned.

**How to spawn the Warden:**
1. Inside an Ancient city, place a Redstone block, then surround it with Soul sand on all four sides, making a small
   cross.
2. Place a candle on top of each Soul sand block.
3. Light all four candles with flint and steel.
4. If you have successfully initiated the spawn, once the last candle is lit, the candles will turn to soul fire.
   Wait about 10 seconds for the Warden to emerge.

Breaking/modifying any part of the structure before the spawn finishes will cancel the ritual.

## Compatible with

This mod is compatible with:
- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric (requires [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api))
- NeoForge
